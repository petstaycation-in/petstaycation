# Booking Operations SOP

This is a local operating checklist. It does not confirm availability, price, policy or payment until the property has replied in writing.

## 1. Qualify the enquiry

- Confirm guest name, phone or WhatsApp, email, travel dates, destination, property, guest count and pet count.
- Ask for each pet's species, breed or type, age, approximate weight, temperament and relevant care needs.
- Record budget, source, preferred unit, meal plan and special requirements.
- Set enquiry status to `New`, then `Qualified` only when the required details are present.

## 2. Confirm the property

- Check the property's verification record and date.
- Ask the owner for current availability, rate, unit, capacity, pet policy, fees, inclusions and cancellation terms.
- Record the evidence source, contact and verification date. Put every missing answer in `unconfirmed fields`.
- Do not describe a rate or pet rule as confirmed when the verification date is missing or stale.

## 3. Approve and send the quote

- Prepare a written proposal with dates, guests, pets, unit, inclusions, rate, taxes or fees, advance, balance due date and cancellation terms.
- Mark the enquiry `Quote sent`. A baseline or historical rate is not a quote.
- Obtain internal approval before sending exceptions, discounts or commission changes.

## 4. Handle advance and payment

- Share payment instructions only through an approved Pet Staycation channel.
- Record requested amount, date, reference, payment status and who verified it.
- Use `Pending` until the payment is matched. Never mark paid from a screenshot alone.

## 5. Confirm the booking

- Send written confirmation only after property acceptance and payment verification.
- Record booking value, commission, Ravindra share, balance due and property contact.
- Set status to `Booked` and retain the final terms with the enquiry.

## 6. Check-in and aftercare

- Reconfirm arrival details and pet disclosure before check-in.
- Follow up after arrival for any urgent issue and record the resolution.
- Request a review after the stay, then record review or referral outcome.

## 7. Cancellation handling

- Acknowledge the request in writing and capture the timestamp.
- Apply only the cancellation terms in the confirmed proposal and ask the property to approve exceptions.
- Record refund due, refund sent, payment status and final enquiry status.

## 8. Commission reconciliation

- Compare the confirmed booking value with the property statement or agreed calculation.
- Record gross booking value, commission basis, commission received and Ravindra share.
- Set reconciliation status to `Pending`, `Partially reconciled` or `Reconciled` and retain the evidence reference.
